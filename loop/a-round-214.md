## Method notes C needs
- **Reset after every drag:**
  - **fade, trim and move:** Ctrl+Z, sent only when the Undo button `title` changed. This means a no-op drag cannot undo the Add.
  - **range:** a no-move click on the range body at the grab point. Escape does not clear a clip range (probe).
  - **Then asserted:** `data-clip-start`, `data-fade-in` and width back to their pre-drag values; `[data-range-start]` = 0; no range label; the Undo `title` back to its pre-drag value.
- **Home (seek 0) before Space and before every playing drag.** The range-reset click moves the playhead, and Follow could otherwise scroll the clip start out of view.
- **Drags:** 60 steps in two halves (30 + 30), +120 px. The mid-drag and end state is read before `mouse.up`. Grab points are checked with `elementFromPoint` and kept inside the viewport.
- **Clip locator:** `data-clip-id`, read after Add. `data-clip-start="0.000"` stops matching during a trim or move drag.
