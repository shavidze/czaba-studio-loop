  - Shots: `shots/hint-zoom-1440.png` (Instrument Sans) vs `shots/hint-zoom-inter-sim-1440.png` (Inter).
  - Caveat: headless Linux rounds advances to whole pixels here. On Windows or macOS the gap may differ a little; that is not verified.
- **Faux bold:** none. Instrument Sans 600/700 and Plex Mono 500 load for real (CDP shows "IBM Plex Mono Medium" on the over-state peak).
- **Mono/sans mix:**
  - `−∞` (master peak at idle, and a track volume at minimum): "−" is Plex Mono and "∞" falls back to DejaVu Sans Mono (C-R151-3). In the shots it looks acceptable at 12 px.

- **Remaining `tabular-nums` text in sans (R5, for B's V-2b):** library durations ("01:41.352"), the library playhead "00:00.000", the zoom readout "40 px/s", the ruler labels and the library counts.
