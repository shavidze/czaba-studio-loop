- **Steps:** on the scratch, change the layout-effect clear guard `if (pending && pending.sequence <= adoptedPreviewSequenceRef.current)` to `if (pending)`. Then run `drag-preview-render` + `status-preview-store`.
- **Expected:** the §1.3 clause ("A newer preview, written after the flush, is kept") is guarded by a row, so the mutant goes red.
- **Actual:** 24/24 green (`mutants/c-summary.txt`). The case is reachable in principle: a pointermove `previewStatus` lands between an async `setStatus` flush and its default-lane commit.
