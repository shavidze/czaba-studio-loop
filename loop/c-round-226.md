## Findings (all Low, for B)

- **C-R226-01 (Low, task spec):** C-BBOX exceptions (a)–(c) do not cover the 1280 lane-fit cascade (above). B should accept it as a deterministic consequence of +20 px or rule otherwise.
- **C-R226-02 (Low, copy):** sheet row `waveform-zoom` shows the key chip "1× button", but the control is a `<select>` ("Waveform Zoom — view only…", options ½×…8×). A user looks for a button. Suggested: "1× menu" or "Waveform zoom list".
- **C-R226-03 (Low, copy):** sheet row `fit` says that double-clicking the ruler or the overview fits the whole project. With a time selection, a ruler double-click zooms to the selection instead (`StudioWorkspace.tsx` `handleRulerDoubleClick` l.1269–1275; the ruler title documents it). Measured: with a selection, ruler double-click 14.1→33.1 px/s and overview 9 px/s. This is an unlisted difference in the sheet.
- **C-R226-04 (Low, pre-existing, frozen files):** a ruler double-click leaves focus on the playhead handle (a `role=slider`). The first click seeks; the second lands on the handle at its new spot, and `ArrangementRuler.tsx` l.151 calls `focus()`. A clip title click does not release it, because `releaseEditFocusOnTimelinePointerDown` (SW l.1612) releases range inputs, text fields, the loop brace and markers, not the playhead slider. The backlog has only a harness note ("ruler clicks at y = ruler bottom − 4 avoid the playhead handle"); C found no ruling on keyboard focus after a clip click (ARCH-CHECKPOINT-SRW14-PREFS (e) is a refactor signal, not a decision). The next ←/→ then moves the playhead by 0.1 s instead of nudging the selected clip, and +/− do nothing (`probe1.json`: after a ruler double-click, `=` leaves 9 px/s unchanged; ArrowRight moves the position 11.200→11.300 and the clip stays). This round did not cause it, but the new sheet now advertises those keys. B decides whether a focus-release rule belongs in CALM-1b or later.
