## Consult: accessibility-auditor on R103 (opinion only, read-only, nothing measured by the auditor)
- The auditor rated all three items **low** conformance risk.
  - **(1) Caret-only indicator after a click:** 2.4.7 is met, because a keyboard mode with a visible indicator exists (Tab, Escape and the other non-editing keys). 2.4.11 is unrelated. 2.4.13 is AAA and only conditional. The auditor's inference: a strict reviewer could still object, since browsers' default heuristics always ring text inputs.
  - **(2) Android `Unidentified`/IME:** the failure is fail-safe (an extra ring, never a lost one) and only breaks the design rule.
  - **(3) AltGr/Option characters:** also fail-safe, but likely the most frequent gap (`@` on German layouts, macOS Option). The auditor suggests `getModifierState('AltGraph')`.
- **Extra suggestion, for Saba/B:** in the pointer rule, `outline: none` also removes the ring in Windows forced-colors mode. `outline-color: transparent` would keep it. This is best practice, not spec text, and it comes from the round-202 design, not from round 203.
- The auditor's "1.5px vs 1px" remark is the known R90 Chromium rounding: the criterion is "≥ 1px painted".
- None of this is a C finding this round. It goes to B as input next to A's M1/I1.
