## What changed
### T1 — `export-wav.client.ts`
- **New exports:**
  - `ARRANGEMENT_WAV_WORKER_STALL_TIMEOUT_MS = 30_000`;
  - `ARRANGEMENT_WAV_WORKER_STALLED_MESSAGE = 'WAV-ის ჩაწერა შეჩერდა — worker 30 წამი არ პასუხობს.'`
- **The timer:**
  - It is a `window.setTimeout` stall timer.
  - It is armed only after `postMessage` returns without throwing. A `postMessage` throw now calls `fail()` and then `return`.
  - `progress` re-arms it, which clears the old timer first.
- **One local `detachWorker()`** is shared by every way the promise ends: complete, error message, onerror, onmessageerror, abort, stall and the postMessage throw. It does four things:
  - clears the timer;
  - removes the abort listener;
  - nulls `onmessage`, `onerror` and `onmessageerror`;
  - calls `terminate()` once.
- **Error text:**
  - `{type:'error', message}` with a non-empty trimmed `message` rejects with `` `${ARRANGEMENT_WAV_WORKER_FAILED_MESSAGE} ${trimmed}` ``.
  - A blank message, `onerror` and `onmessageerror` (now `() => fail()`), a postMessage throw and a constructor throw keep the exact constant.
- **Unchanged:** the signature, the pre-abort check, the no-Worker fallback and the constructor-catch message.
- **Change after review:** in the first version, only abort and stall nulled the handlers. The code-reviewer found that a late `progress` after `fail` or `complete` would re-arm the timer and call `terminate()` a second time 30 s later, and would also call `onProgress` after the export had settled. `detachWorker` closes this, and EW8c/EW8e now test it (mutation m8). This extends B's text ("Clear it on …") without contradicting it: the main paths behave the same.
