Our Story

CZABA was born from a simple frustration: finding high-quality, truly cinematic sounds that don't break the bank or come with complicated licensing.

As composers and sound designers ourselves, we spent years building sample libraries for our own projects. Friends started asking where we got our sounds. That's when we realized we had something special.

Today, CZABA serves thousands of producers, artists, and creators worldwide. Every sound in our library is crafted with the same attention to detail we bring to our own cinematic work.
